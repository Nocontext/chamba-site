Program

Chamba Messaging is a text messaging program from Chamba Development, LLC. Messages may be labeled Chamba, BinCard, or LandScope.

Program description

Chamba Messaging sends one-time passcodes, account and status notifications, error alerts, reminders, and replies to questions. Messages are sent only to people who have opted in, including household members, team members, and users of our apps.

Rates and frequency

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Help and opt-out

Reply HELP for help.

Reply STOP to opt out. After you reply STOP, you will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent.
