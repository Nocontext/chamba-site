Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Chamba Development, LLC (“Chamba,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our software, services, and apps, including BinCard and LandScope, and our text messaging program, Chamba Messaging.

Information we collect

  • Your name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your email address
  • The content of messages exchanged between you and us
  • App usage information needed to provide the service

How we use your information

We use this information only to provide and support our software and services, including to:

  • Deliver one-time passcodes (OTP) for signing in
  • Send account and status notifications
  • Send error alerts
  • Send reminders
  • Reply to your questions and support requests

SMS opt-in data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service providers

We use Twilio as our SMS service provider. Twilio processes phone numbers and message content only to deliver messages on our behalf.

Text messaging choices

You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. See our SMS Terms and Conditions for details.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
